Privacy & data handling

We never accept full Social Security numbers. Please send an export with only the last 4 digits of Social Security numbers and no home addresses; we leave placeholders you fill in before filing.

  • We only use your file to prepare your report and never share or sell it.
  • Files are moved out of email into encrypted storage as soon as they arrive. Payroll contents are never copied into chat, tickets or logs.
  • Any file containing full Social Security numbers is deleted, and we ask you for a last-4 version.
  • We delete your file within 30 days. You keep the official records required by RCW 39.12.120.
  • If we ever suspect unauthorized access to your data, we notify you immediately.
