Privacy Policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

What we collect

Contact details you send us, billing information processed by our payment provider (Stripe; we don't store card numbers), and store/listing data we access while performing the service.

How we use it

Only to provide the service, communicate with you, and bill you. We don't sell your data.

Sharing

Only with providers needed to run the business (e.g. payment processing, email) or when required by law.

Retention & deletion

We keep data while you're a customer and as required for records. Revoke our access anytime, and email us to request deletion.

Certified Payroll Desk: payroll files

We never accept full Social Security numbers. Send payroll exports with only the last 4 digits of Social Security numbers and no home addresses. We leave placeholders that you fill in before filing.

  • Use: we only use your file to prepare your certified payroll report. We never share, sell or use it for anything else.
  • Storage: files are moved out of email into encrypted storage when they arrive, with access limited to Vemparty. We never copy payroll contents into chat, tickets or logs.
  • Full SSNs: if a file contains full Social Security numbers, we delete it and ask you for a last-4 version.
  • Retention: we delete your file within 30 days of receipt. Reports we return show only the last 4 digits. Under RCW 39.12.120 you keep the official payroll records.
  • Incidents: if we suspect unauthorized access to your data, we notify you immediately and help with any notices required under RCW 19.255.
