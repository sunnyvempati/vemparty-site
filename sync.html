FAQ

How do you access my accounts? Is it secure?

We ask for limited staff or user permissions you create and can revoke at any time. We never ask for your passwords, and we never handle payouts or banking settings.

Can you prevent marketplace suspensions or policy actions?

No. Each marketplace controls its own account actions, reviews and policies. We help keep your data consistent but cannot control or guarantee marketplace decisions.
